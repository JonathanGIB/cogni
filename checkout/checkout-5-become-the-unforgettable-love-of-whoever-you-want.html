FAQ About Payments

Crypto payments?

Content platforms such as Google Books or Amazon Books, Card payment processors, and Buy Now, Pay Later service providers, impose extremely high fees, ranging from 45% to 80% of our revenue for each sale. If we decided to accept these payment methods, we would have to increase the prices of our eBooks, courses, seminars and other content by more than three times, which would directly impact our customers.

By opting exclusively for USDC, we can maintain fair and affordable prices without having to inflate them to cover external fees.

I am new at crypto payments. Is it complicated to pay with USDC?

Absolutely not. If you have not yet used USDC, you can easily buy and transfer from most exchanges. Simply select the USDC amount, our Wallet address, the Ethereum network and you're done. You can use platforms such as MoonPay, Coinbase and Binance, as they allow you to buy USDC easily and quickly using Google Pay, Apple pay, Debit and Credit Cards, Paypal, GiftCards, etc, with 0 fees.
