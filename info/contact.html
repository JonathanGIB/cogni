Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to create an account to buy an eBook?

You do not need to register or create an account to purchase our ebooks and other digital products. Just click on the purchase button and proceed to checkout. After receiving your payment you will be redirected to the download link of your files. A copy of your files will also be sent to your email, as well as your payment and billing information.

How can I become an affiliate and receive a percentage of the sales generated with my code?

The affiliate program is in beta stage and we will release more details soon. You can send us an email to partners@cognitifly.com providing relevant information about your numbers and social media niche. Once your profile has been analyzed, and if accepted, affiliates will be able to receive up to 70% of the sales generated with their personal codes.

Do you offer refunds?

If you meet all the requirements of our return policy for non-digital (printed) products you can send an email to help@cognitifly.com requesting your refund. Within 72 hours we will contact you to evaluate and execute your case.

How do I participate in the monthly giveaway?

Every eBook purchase automatically enters you into our monthly giveaway for a Kindle Paperwhite or iPad Pro. The more eBooks you purchase, the higher your chances of winning. Winners are announced on the first day of each month. Your participation number, which will be used to claim your prize in the giveaway, will be the same as the license number you acquire for each book purchased. This license will be downloaded along with the digital files of your purchase.
