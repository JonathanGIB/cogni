Community Manager

As our Community Manager, you'll be the voice and face of Cognitifly across all social platforms. You'll build relationships with our readers, create engaging content, and help grow our online presence while maintaining our brand identity.

Key Requirements:

  • Advanced handling of social media platforms (Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, etc.)
  • Skills for metrics analysis
  • Knowledge of digital marketing strategies and social media advertising
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills
  • Creative writing and storytelling skills

Data Analyst

The Data Analyst will be a key technical contributor within the Data Analytics function of the Customer Care Operations organization. You will demonstrate deep expertise in querying, structuring, and visualizing actionable data to drive operational improvements for customer success.

Technical Skills:

  • Databases
  • Spreadsheets
  • Statistics
  • Data visualization tools, preferably MS Power BI, Google Looker
  • Data manipulation and processing, preferably in SQL

Additional Requirements:

  • Understanding of relevant KPIs and processes
  • Passion for solving highly technical business problems through effective data manipulation and visualization
  • Ability to thrive in a fast-paced environment
  • Eagerness to tackle diverse data projects
